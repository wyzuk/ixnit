# Recommended Books & Learning Material

Books and courses that shaped my mental models as a software engineer.

## Books
1. **"You Don't Know JS" (Kyle Simpson)**
   * Excellent deep dive into scopes, closures, objects, prototypes, and asynchronous JavaScript.
2. **"Designing Data-Intensive Applications" (Martin Kleppmann)**
   * The gold standard for distributed systems, replication, partitioning, transactions, and consensus.
3. **"Clean Code" (Robert C. Martin)**
   * Great principles on naming, small functions, and readability (even if some parts are a bit dated).
4. **"The Pragmatic Programmer" (David Thomas, Andrew Hunt)**
   * Mindset shifts on developer ergonomics, automation, testing, and career longevity.

## Interactive Learning
* **Execute Program (Gary Bernhardt)**: Concise interactive lessons on TypeScript, SQL, Modern JS, and Git.
* **CS50 (Harvard)**: Timeless introduction to computer science fundamentals, memory, and algorithms.
